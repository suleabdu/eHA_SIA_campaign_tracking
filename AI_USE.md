Used Claude (Anthropic) to convert the approved paper questionnaire (Household_Questionnaire_HH2026v1.docx) and the six reference-media CSVs into the XLSForm — all constraint, relevance, and calculation logic was AI-drafted from the requirements I gave it, then reviewed against the paper form line by line.
The build script (build_form.py) and its output were AI-generated but not accepted on trust: every version was recompiled with pyxform 4.5.0 and checked against ODK Validate before being treated as correct. One real bug (an indexed-repeat() call using unexpanded field names, which would have silently broken the roster→child-module linkage) was caught this way and fixed, not shipped.
The defect analysis in Design_Documentation.md — the age-sentinel collision, the missing DK code on 6.01/6.02, the duplicate PIN in staff_roster.csv, the antibiotic list referenced but absent from the data pack — was produced by the AI reading the source files directly, not asserted from general knowledge; each finding is traceable to a specific cell/row in the provided files.
check_digit.py and test_plan.csv are AI-written; the check-digit logic was independently validated by running it (all cases pass, including the transposition-detection proof) rather than taken as self-evidently correct.
The Hausa labels in the form are AI-drafted and explicitly flagged in the documentation as an unvalidated working translation, not a certified, back-translated survey instrument — disclosed rather than presented as field-ready.
